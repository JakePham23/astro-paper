# Book Review Template & Reading Tag System

Use this file as the standard template to duplicate when writing a new book review under `src/content/posts/en/10-review-sach/`.

## Tag System for Book Reviews

- `#book-review` — Overall evaluation, impressions, and rating
- `#book-summary` — Core content summarized chapter by chapter
- `#key-takeaways` — Actionable lessons you can apply immediately at work or in life
- `#notable-quotes` — Memorable quotes and passages worth saving
- `#tech-books` — Books on programming, System Design, Architecture, AI/Engineering
- `#mindset-books` — Books on mindset, management, productivity, and soft skills
